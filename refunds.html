Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Zenvora Digital sells digital ebook downloads. Please review the product description and ensure your device supports the file format before purchasing. This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection law.

1. Change-of-mind requests

Because ebooks are digital products that may be accessed or downloaded immediately, we generally cannot offer a change-of-mind refund once the digital file has been successfully delivered or accessed, except where required by applicable law. Please check the title, description, price, and format before completing checkout.

2. When you should contact us

Please contact us if any of the following happens:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Payment succeeded but delivery failed: your payment was captured but no download link was provided, or the link does not work.
  • File problem: the supplied file is corrupted, cannot be opened despite using a compatible reader, or is materially different from the product description.
  • Unauthorised transaction: you believe a purchase was made without your permission. Also contact your payment provider promptly.

We will review the order and payment records. If a refund or replacement is appropriate, we will explain the next steps. Refunds for eligible transactions are returned through the payment provider's process and timing; the time for funds to appear can depend on your bank or payment method.

3. How to request help

Email specialpages.in@gmail.com or call +91 60062 08284. Include your order number, the email address used at checkout, the ebook title, the payment date, and a short description of the issue. Do not send your full card details, UPI PIN, password, or OTP.

For payment issues, a payment confirmation or transaction reference may help us investigate. Please do not email sensitive payment credentials.

4. Cancellation

Orders for instant digital delivery may begin processing as soon as payment is verified, so cancellation may not be possible after delivery or access. If you paid by mistake or delivery has not completed, contact us as soon as possible and we will review the circumstances.

5. Processing and legal rights

We assess requests individually and may ask for reasonable information to verify the transaction. Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law. Where this policy conflicts with mandatory law, the applicable law will prevail.
