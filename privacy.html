Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Information we may handle

Depending on how you use the store, we may handle information you provide when placing an order or contacting support, such as your name, email address, order details, and messages. Payment details are handled by the payment provider; we do not ask you to send your UPI PIN, card PIN, password, CVV, or OTP by email or phone.

2. Payment and order information

To process purchases and provide digital downloads, the store may use order identifiers, ebook details, payment-provider references, payment status, and the email address supplied at checkout. Payment credentials are processed through the payment provider's checkout rather than collected in support messages.

3. How information is used

  • To create and manage orders and verify payment status.
  • To provide download access and help resolve delivery or payment problems.
  • To respond to customer support requests and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to run the store, such as hosting, database/storage, payment processing, or transactional email providers. These providers handle information under their own terms and applicable safeguards. We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legitimate rights and security.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to order information should be limited to people who need it to operate the store and support customers.

6. Retention

Order and transaction records may be retained for customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance for as long as reasonably necessary. Support messages may be retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain relevant records.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with an order, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate transaction records. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Cookies and analytics

The website may use browser storage for essential features such as remembering items in your cart. If analytics or other optional tracking tools are introduced, this policy should be updated to identify them and explain available choices.

9. Children's privacy

The store is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the store, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
